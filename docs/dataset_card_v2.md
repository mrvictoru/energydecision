# AEMO Battery Trading Dataset v2

Offline reinforcement learning dataset for battery energy storage trading in
Australia's National Electricity Market (NEM). **77 million timesteps** across
**2,400 episodes** from 6 distinct policies, 4 battery configurations,
5 NEM regions, and 3 episode horizons.

This dataset replaces the original v1 (all 0.5C batteries) with realistic
power-to-capacity ratios matching real-world BESS stations.

## Dataset Structure

Parquet file with 6 columns:

| Column | Type | Description |
|--------|------|-------------|
| `step` | Int64 | Timestep index within the episode |
| `norm_observation` | List(Float32) | Normalised 18-dim state vector (see below) |
| `action` | List(Float32) | 9-dim action: energy dispatch + 8 FCAS bids |
| `reward` | Float32 | Step reward (energy + FCAS revenue − degradation cost) |
| `episode_id` | Int32 | Unique episode identifier |
| `source_policy` | String | Policy that generated the episode |

### State Space (18-dim)

Normalised AEMO market features:

1–7: Energy price (RRP), FCAS prices (REG, 6s, 60s, 5min for raise/lower)
8–9: Solar and wind generation as % of regional capacity
10: Total demand
11: Battery state of charge
12–17: Time features (hour sin/cos, day sin/cos, is_peak, battery flow)

### Action Space (9-dim, `full_fcas` mode)

| Dim | Description | Range |
|:---:|-------------|:-----:|
| 0 | Energy dispatch (−1 = charge, +1 = discharge) | [−1, 1] |
| 1–8 | FCAS service bids (8 services: RAISEREG, LOWERREG, RAISE6SEC, LOWER6SEC, RAISE60SEC, LOWER60SEC, RAISE5MIN, LOWER5MIN) | [0, 1] |

## Battery Configurations

Four battery variants matching real-world Australian BESS stations:

| Battery | Capacity | Max Power | C-rate | Duration | Real-world match |
|---------|:--------:|:---------:|:------:|:--------:|:-----------------|
| `medium_1c` | 10 MWh | 10 MW | **1.0C** | 60 min | Torrens Island, Waratah, Lake Bonney |
| `large_07c` | 50 MWh | 35 MW | **0.7C** | 86 min | Hornsdale, Victorian Big Battery |
| `small_05c` | 2 MWh | 1 MW | **0.5C** | 120 min | Kennedy Energy Park (legacy) |
| `fast_375c` | 8 MWh | 30 MW | **3.75C** | 16 min | Dalrymple North BESS |

Distribution: `medium_1c` 40%, `large_07c` 25%, `small_05c` 20%, `fast_375c` 15%.

## Source Policies

| Policy | Episodes | Algorithm | Description |
|--------|:--------:|-----------|-------------|
| PPO | 900 | Proximal Policy Optimisation | Trained on combined 5-region data |
| TD3 | 300 | Twin Delayed DDPG | Off-policy continuous control |
| A2C | 300 | Advantage Actor-Critic | On-policy with parallel actors |
| DDPG | 300 | Deep Deterministic PG | Off-policy deterministic |
| SAC | 300 | Soft Actor-Critic | Maximum-entropy off-policy |
| FCAS rule | 300 | Heuristic rule | Percentile-based FCAS bidding on energy dispatch baseline |

All SB3 models were trained on the combined 5-region dataset with all 4 battery
configurations at 5-minute resolution.

## Episode Horizons

All three horizons are equally distributed (33% each):

| Horizon | Steps | Duration at 5-min resolution |
|:-------:|:-----:|:----------------------------|
| Short | 3,456 | 12 days |
| Medium | 16,128 | 8 weeks |
| Long | 74,880 | 26 weeks |

## Regions

5 NEM regions, 480 episodes each:

| Region | States covered | Date range |
|--------|---------------|------------|
| NSW1 | New South Wales | 2021-01 — 2023-04 |
| QLD1 | Queensland | 2021-01 — 2023-04 |
| SA1 | South Australia | 2022-04 — 2023-12 |
| TAS1 | Tasmania | 2021-01 — 2023-04 |
| VIC1 | Victoria | 2021-04 — 2023-12 |

## Data Generation

Generated by `src/generate_fcas_dataset.py` from the
[energydecision](https://github.com/mrvictoru/energydecision) repository.

```bash
# Train SB3 models on all 4 batteries
python3 scripts/retrain_sb3_full_fcas.py --algorithms PPO,A2C,DDPG,SAC,TD3

# Generate episodes
python3 src/generate_fcas_dataset.py --policies ppo,td3,a2c,ddpg,sac,fcas_rule \
  --output-dir data/aemo_dt_fcas_v2

# Assemble final dataset
python3 src/generate_fcas_dataset.py --mode assemble --output-dir data/aemo_dt_fcas_v2
```

## Recommended Use

This dataset is designed for offline RL training of Decision Transformers
for battery trading in the NEM. The 9-dim `full_fcas` action space enables
simultaneous energy arbitrage and FCAS market participation.

The recommended model architecture matching this dataset:

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| state_dim | 18 |
| act_dim | 9 |
| context_len | 180 |
| step_duration | 5 minutes |
