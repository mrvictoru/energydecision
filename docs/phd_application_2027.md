## 2. The structural finding that drives the whole plan

At November 2026, most 2027 Semester 1 international scholarship rounds at Australian Group of Eight universities have closed. For an international applicant without an Australian or New Zealand degree, there are only four realistic funded routes:

1. **A university international scholarship round** — a small, school-nominated quota; highly competitive.
2. **An advertised project-funded place** — money already held by a supervisor from ARC / ARENA / CRC / industry. This is the highest-probability route for this profile.
3. **A CRC or agency industry-PhD scheme** — CSIRO iPhD, RACE for 2030.
4. **New Zealand** — international PhD students pay domestic tuition (see §6), and doctoral scholarships cover stipend plus fees.

The practical consequence is that this is not a search for "a PhD place"; it is a search for **one of a small number of international nominations, or an advertised funded project**. Three deadlines fall within the next nine weeks: **15 January, 17 January and 1 February 2027**.

**Evidence convention used throughout:** "verified" means an official page or a reputable outlet; "third-party" means an aggregator and is indicative only; "unverified" means no reliable source was found. Stipend figures marked (T) are third-party.

## 9. Application preparation plan (while preprint endorsement is pending)

### 9.1 The key reframe
An arXiv endorsement is a **credibility/visibility dependency, not an application dependency**. arXiv provides a date-stamped public record, a citable URL and discoverability; none is required to lodge an application. Selection panels rank on prior academic results and research potential, with refereed publications one *considered factor* among others. Endorsement is not peer review.

**Do not wait for it.** The binding constraints are three deadlines in nine weeks and three documents with long lead times (English test, transcripts, referee letters).

### 9.2 Immediate workaround, no endorsement needed
- Tag a GitHub release and mint a **Zenodo DOI** for the paper, the code, and (separately) the model weights / dataset. This gives a dated, versioned, citable public record immediately.
- Add `CITATION.cff` to the repository and a short paper/claim-set document with a frozen, explicit list of claims.
- Citation line for the CV: *"…, technical report / preprint, Zenodo DOI …, v1, Nov 2026. Not peer reviewed."*
- When arXiv clears, add the arXiv ID to the **same version**. Nothing else in the pack changes.

### 9.3 Endorsement facts (verified, official arXiv announcement 21 Jan 2026)
arXiv no longer accepts an institutional email address as the sole qualifier for new submitters. Two routes remain:
1. an academic/research institutional email **plus** prior authorship on a paper already accepted in that arXiv endorsement domain, claimed in the arXiv account; or
2. a **personal endorsement** from an established arXiv author in the same domain.

After a submission is started, arXiv emails a six-character code that the endorser enters. Practical guidance: use a former supervisor, mentor or co-author who has published in the relevant category; send them the PDF first; arXiv explicitly asks people **not** to mass-email potential endorsers. Do not make a cold endorsement request the opening contact with a prospective supervisor.

### 9.4 Build the evidence pack
| Item | Definition of done |
|---|---|
| One-command reproduction | Clone → results table in one command; pinned release; locked environment; expected runtime; one-GPU requirement stated |
| Results sheet | Four held-out surfaces + nine-cell impact gate with exact numbers, in one table; simulated vs real-telemetry data labelled |
| Limitations box | Simulator-based; preprint; single home GPU; piecewise merit-order impact model; no live shadow/deployment — state these before a reviewer does |
| Short screen capture | 3–5 minutes of the evaluator actually running |
| CV, summary, proposals | 2-page CV; 1-page plain summary; three 2-page proposal variants |
| Admin | Transcripts + grading-scale note; English test booked; two referees briefed with paper, CV and deadline list |

### 9.5 Where to spend time
Approximately 50% supervisor contact and funding-route confirmation, 30% the pack, 10% the endorsement, 10% admin. For an international applicant the scarce resource is **a school nomination**, not a preprint; a supervisor who has read the code and wants the applicant in their group is worth more than an arXiv ID. **Do not start a new experiment now** — freeze the claim set, since a half-finished result is a liability at 17 Jan.
